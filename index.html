Actionable Strategic Recommendations

Business analyst roadmap to optimize margins, table turn, and portfolio profitability.

1. Mexican Menu Revamp & SKU Rationalization

Three out of the bottom five revenue items belong to the Mexican line (Chicken Tacos at only 123 sold). Re-engineer recipe appeal or replace underperforming items with high-margin combos to reduce waste and prep complexity.

2. Dual-Peak Kitchen Staffing & Production Optimization

Over 48% of gross daily revenue concentrates within two narrow 2-hour windows (12 PM – 2 PM lunch rush at $42.3K and 5 PM – 7 PM dinner rush at $34.7K). Shift prep shifts forward to 11:00 AM and 4:30 PM to avoid service bottlenecks.

3. Bundle High-Volume Anchors to Expand AOV Beyond $29.65

High-volume low-cost items like Hamburgers (622 units) and Edamame (620 units) are ordered regularly. Bundle them with high-margin Italian entrees or Asian specialty drinks to systematically nudge average ticket size toward $35.00+.
